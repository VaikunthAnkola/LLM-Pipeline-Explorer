How it works

Step 1: Tokenize

Text is split into tokens (whole words or subword pieces like prefixes and suffixes).

Step 2: Assign IDs

Each token becomes a number ID from the vocabulary so the computer can process it mathematically.

Step 3: Embed in 3D

Tokens are converted to coordinate vectors, where sentences with similar meanings land close together in 3D space.

Real LLMs vs this demo

Massive Vocabularies

Real models use subword vocabularies containing tens of thousands of tokens trained across entire languages.

High-Dimensional Vectors

Real models learn embeddings with hundreds or thousands of numbers per token (e.g. 768 to 4096 dimensions), not just 3.

Learned from Big Data

Real models learn semantic patterns and contextual probabilities from trillions of tokens of text rather than a small hand-written table.
